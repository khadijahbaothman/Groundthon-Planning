Mentor Selection Criteria

  • Relevant expertise: practical guidance in assigned area.
  • Availability: can commit to the mentoring window.
  • Practical perspective: can challenge feasibility, not only concept quality.
  • Neutrality: no conflict affecting fair support.
  • Communication: clear, constructive and actionable feedback.

Suggested Expertise Coverage

Operations • Baggage / GSE / Turnaround • AI / Digital Solutions • Data / Analytics • Facility • Sustainability • Business / Finance • HR • Commercial
